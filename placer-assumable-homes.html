How taking over a loan works

  1. Keep the seller's rateYou take over their loan balance, rate and remaining years, often under 4%.
  2. Cover the cash gapPay the difference between the price and the balance in cash, or put part of it on a 2nd loan.
  3. Get the lender's OKFHA buyers must live in the home. Anyone who qualifies can take over a VA loan, though some sellers want a veteran.
  4. Plan for 45 to 90 daysThe seller's loan servicer approves you, so closing takes longer than usual.
