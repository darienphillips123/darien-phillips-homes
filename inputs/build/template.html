      How taking over a loan works

      1. Keep the seller's rateYou take over their balance, rate and remaining years.
      2. Cover the cash gapPay the difference between the price and the balance in cash or with a 2nd loan.
      3. Get the lender's OKFHA buyers must live in the home. Anyone who qualifies can take over a VA loan, though some sellers want a veteran.
      4. Plan for 45 to 90 daysThe seller's loan servicer has to approve you, so closing takes longer.
